1. Introduction

Hatırlatıcı ("the app") is a personal reminder app designed to send you reminders at the times you choose. This document explains what information is collected while you use the app, how it is used and how it is protected.

By downloading and using the app, you agree to this policy. Please read it carefully before using the app.

2. Data controller

The app is developed and published by Abdulkadir Işıktan as an independent developer. Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), the developer is the data controller for the limited data processed by the app. Contact details are provided at the end of this document.

3. Information collected

3.1 Information you store on your device

You do not need to create an account to use the app. The following information you enter in the app is kept only in a local database (Room) on your device and is not sent to the developer or any third party:

  • Reminder title, type and note
  • Total and remaining count
  • Reminder times, repeat days and duration
  • Reminder history (taken / skipped / snoozed status and time)

3.2 Automatically collected usage data

Google Firebase Analytics is used to improve the app. This service collects events such as app opens, screen views and general feature usage, together with identifiers such as device model, operating system version and Firebase Installation ID. This data is aggregated and statistical; nothing about the content of your reminders or your personal information is transmitted this way.

3.3 Remote configuration

Firebase Remote Config is used to update app features from the server (for example, turning certain features such as the history view window or the widget on or off). During this process, an anonymous installation ID assigned to your device communicates with Google's servers; no personal data is sent.

4. Permissions

The app requests the following permissions for its reminder functionality. None of these permissions are used to collect personal data:

PermissionPurpose
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)Show a notification when a reminder is due
Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)Trigger reminders at the exact time
Full-screen notifications (USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT)Open the reminder screen on the lock screen
Boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)Reschedule alarms after the phone restarts
Vibrate / keep awake (VIBRATE, WAKE_LOCK)Briefly wake the device while showing a notification

The app does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts or photos.

5. Purposes of use

  • Showing the right reminder at the right time
  • Calculating and showing you the remaining count
  • Measuring the stability and usability of the app (with anonymous analytics data)
  • Determining which features are enabled for you (via remote configuration)

6. Sharing and third parties

Your reminder data is never shared with third parties, sold or used for advertising. The app contains no ad networks.

The app uses the following sub-processor (third-party) service:

ServicePurposePolicy
Google Firebase
Analytics · Remote Config		 Anonymous usage statistics and remote feature configuration policies.google.com/privacy

7. Retention and deletion

Your reminder data is kept on your device until you delete it within the app or uninstall the app. When you uninstall the app, this data is completely removed from your device; the developer keeps no copy.

Anonymous usage data collected by Firebase Analytics is automatically deleted or anonymized after a certain period in accordance with Google's own retention policies.

8. Data security

Never sending your data to servers is the strongest form of protection. Data on your device is kept within the operating system's app sandbox and is accessible only to someone who can unlock the device. Communication with Google Firebase takes place over an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS).

9. Backup

If Android's automatic backup feature is enabled, your app data (including your reminder records) may be backed up by Android to the cloud linked to your own Google account. This backup takes place on Google's infrastructure under your own account; the developer cannot access these backups. You can turn this feature off in your device settings.

10. Children's privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you become aware that the app is being used by a child, please contact us.

11. Your rights

Under Article 11 of KVKK and related legislation, you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, to request information, and to request its correction or deletion. Since in-app data is already on your device, you can exercise these rights directly by deleting the relevant record in the app or uninstalling the app. To request deletion of the identifier associated with you in Firebase Analytics, you can contact us at the address below.

12. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. When there are significant changes, a notice will be shown in the app or the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. Continuing to use the app after changes means you accept the current policy.
