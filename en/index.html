Small, but complete

Everything you need and nothing you don't. Open the app and you instantly see what to do next.

  • Right-on-time alarms

    Reminders ring to the minute. They open full screen even when your phone is locked, and are rescheduled automatically after a restart.

  • One-tap check-off

    Tap "Done" or "Snooze" right from the notification and let the app handle the rest. Tapped by mistake? Undo it.

  • Flexible scheduling

    Multiple times a day, specific weekdays, a set duration and meal timing (before / after meals). It fits any routine.

  • Stock tracking

    The remaining count goes down automatically each time. You get a heads-up when it runs low or runs out.

  • History and adherence

    See what you completed, skipped or snoozed over the last 30 days on one screen, along with your adherence rate.

  • Home screen widget

    See today's plan and your progress on the home screen without opening the app.

Common questions

Didn't find your answer? Email us.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Reminders are scheduled and run entirely on your device. Your alarms ring on time even when you're offline.

Is my data sent to a server?

Your reminders are never sent to any server. Only anonymous, aggregated usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) are collected to improve the app, and they never include the content of your reminders.

Will my alarms be lost if my phone restarts?

No. When your device boots up, Hatırlatıcı reschedules all alarms automatically.

Why might a reminder be late?

Battery saver modes on some phones can restrict apps running in the background. The app warns you if notification or exact alarm permissions are turned off; we recommend enabling them and excluding Hatırlatıcı from battery optimization.

What happens to my data if I uninstall the app?

Uninstalling the app deletes all reminder records on your device. The developer keeps no copy.
